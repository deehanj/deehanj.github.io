Privacy Policy for Handsfree Golf Club

Last updated: January 10, 2026

Developer: James Deehan

This privacy policy explains how Handsfree Golf Club ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our service:

  • User ID: When you create an account through our authentication provider (Auth0), we receive and store a unique user identifier. We do not collect your email address, name, or other personal details from the authentication process.
  • Scorecard Data: We store the golf scorecard information you enter into the App.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Identify your account and associate it with your scorecards
  • Store and retrieve your scorecard history
  • Provide the core functionality of the App

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on servers hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in accordance with industry-standard security practices. While the App functions offline, your data syncs to our secure servers when a connection is available.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. We use Auth0 solely for authentication purposes; they do not receive your scorecard data.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please contact us at your@email.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at your@email.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or other means.
